    Quick Reference

    Hexes are 6 miles across. PCs and mounts can travel up to 8 hours a day. One clear terrain hex takes 2 hours to cross.

    Travel Procedure
    1. The GM determines and shares the weather.
    2. The GM determines and shares visible hexes and their danger levels.
    3. The GM rolls a d6 for each newly visible hex. On a 1, it has a point of interest (pg. 25). If it is visible, the GM shares this information.
    4. The PCs make task checks in any order they choose.
    5. The PCs choose a direction and a number of hexes to move, and move accordingly.
    6. The GM checks for a random encounter in every other hex using the hex's danger level.
    7. Each time the PCs enter a hex, the GM repeats steps 2-3.
    8. Repeat steps 4-8 until all hex travel is completed.
    9. When all hex travel is completed, go to Camping on pg. 25.
    Travel Tasks

    Each PC can attempt one task per day. PCs can do the same task. Each task effect is limited to once per day. The GM sets the DC based on the situation (default DC 12).

    • Forecast. INT or WIS. Treat the weather as one step better until the next rest.
    • March Song. CHA. Grant one luck token.
    • Push. CON or CHA. Increase base hexes per day by 50%. PCs have DISADV on CON checks until the end of the next rest.
    • Scout. DEX or WIS. Reduce the danger level of all hexes by one step until the next rest (minimum unsafe).
    • Trailblaze. STR or INT. For each PC who succeeds, reduce the terrain type of 1d4 adjacent hexes by one step. Can't be used on impassable terrain. The PCs choose which terrain to affect as they travel. Lasts until the next rest.
    Camping

    PCs can camp during a rest when outside a settlement. Use standard resting rules (Shadowdark RPG pg. 86). Camping without a campfire gives DISADV on all checks.

    Each PC can do one task while camping and still get a full rest. PCs can do the same task. Default DC 12.

    • Batten Down. INT or CON. Treat the weather as one step better until the end of the rest. Limit 1/day.
    • Cook. INT or WIS. Each PC that consumes a ration wakes up with +2 temporary HP (maximum 6).
    • Craft. DEX. Create 2d4 pieces of mundane weapon ammunition.
    • Entertain. CHA. Grant 1 luck token.
    • Firewood. STR or CON. Make one free campfire this rest.
    • Hunt. STR or DEX. The group gains the benefit of consuming rations without expending any.
    • Keep Watch. WIS. Reduce the danger level of all hexes by one step this rest (minimum unsafe).
    • Predict. INT or WIS. Learn the weather for the next 3 days.

    Procedure. 1. The PCs make task checks in any order. 2. The GM checks for two random encounters. 3. If an encounter occurs, PCs who chose Keep Watch may make DC 12 WIS checks to not be surprised.

    Fast Travel

    PCs can fast travel between two hexes they have previously entered, along a path they have already traveled, or between two hexes with a clear path such as a road. Distances are abstract; the GM factors in the travel method. Close is 1-3 days away, Near is up to a week, Far is up to two weeks.

    Distance Rations Time Encounter
    Close None 1d4 days 2:6
    Near 1d4 per PC 2d4 days 4:6
    Far 2d4 per PC 4d4 days 5:6
    1. The GM determines distance, rations, and time. The PCs pay ration costs immediately and must permanently sacrifice one item for each ration they lack (bundled gear such as arrows counts as one item).
    2. The GM checks if one random encounter occurs using the listed probability. If so, the GM picks a random day and time for it.
    3. After resolving any encounters, the PCs arrive.
    Safe Hexes

    The PCs must declare they want to make a hex safe. The GM then generates threats based on danger level: Unsafe 1, Risky 2, Deadly 3. A dangerous point of interest is the first threat. Remaining threats are generated on pg. 32.

    If the PCs alleviate each threat, the hex becomes safe. It stays safe while the PCs or their allies occupy it. Otherwise it stays safe for a number of weeks equal to the average party level, and reentering resets the timer. When the timer runs out, the hex reverts to a random danger level (pg. 24, reroll result 6-8).

    Weather Tables

    Treat the weather steps as a ladder with fair at the center. Roll each day on the season's table and adjust the current weather. It lasts until the end of the next rest. Visibility is the distance in hexes the PCs can see (+2 at mid elevations, +5 at high). Terrain raises the terrain type by the steps shown. PCs must pass the listed CON check to benefit from rest.

    Food and Water

    PCs and mounts can go three days without consuming a ration. They take 1 CON damage each day after that (death at 0). Mounts find their rations during a rest unless in a barren location.

    Downtime Rules

    PCs do one downtime activity between adventures. Activities marked * cost 10 gp x PC level. The PC must pass the check, and can't use luck tokens. After a failed check, reduce that DC one step next time (reset after success).

    Downtime: Spiritualism

    WIS check. Meditation, prayer, self-reflection, or mystical study.

    • DC 9: Gain favor with a local church. Your renown increases by 1 point.
    • DC 12: Spiritual strengthening. You gain 2 XP.
    • DC 15*: Find personal insight. Reroll one prior talent roll.
    • DC 18*: Spiritual cleansing. End one curse afflicting you.
    Downtime: Skulduggery

    Thievery, criminal activity, or churning the rumor mill.

    CHA check

    • DC 9: Start a rumor about one person. Their renown increases or decreases by 1 point (you choose).
    • DC 12: Lay low. Escape the City Guard over a minor crime.
    • DC 15: Extortion. Increase the value of your next sale by 25%, or reduce the cost of your next purchase by 25%.
    • DC 18: Hide out. Escape the City Guard over a major crime.

    DEX check

    • DC 15*: Get away with a minor crime, such as petty theft. If you fail, the City Guard investigates (pg. 45).
    • DC 18*: Get away with a major crime, such as murder. If you fail, the City Guard investigates (pg. 45).
    Downtime: Martial Training

    Weapons and armor training by a suitable expert. Training depends on the PC's class hit points die. INT, STR, or DEX check.

    d4

    • DC 15*: +1 to hit or damage with one weapon. Limit once of each per weapon.
    • DC 18*: Learn to wield a new weapon (d6 damage max).

    d6

    • DC 12*: +1 to hit and damage with one weapon. Limit once per weapon.
    • DC 15*: Learn to wield a new weapon (d10 damage max) or the next step up in armor.

    d8+

    • DC 9*: Learn to wield a new armor or weapon.
    • DC 12*: +1 to hit and damage with one weapon. Limit once per weapon.
    • DC 15*: Increase the damage die of one weapon by one step (d12 damage max). Limit twice per weapon.
    Downtime: Magical Research

    Studying spells, praying to the gods, or consulting oracles. Spellcasters make spellcasting checks.

    INT or CHA spellcasters

    • DC 12: Gain ADV on your next check to learn from a scroll.
    • DC 15*: Create a scroll of one spell you know of tier 3 or less.
    • DC 15*: Create one Potion: Flying, Forgetfulness, Giant Strength, Invisibility, or Polymorph.
    • DC 20*: Create a wand of a spell you know of tier 3 or less. You can't create a new wand of the same spell until the prior wand breaks.

    WIS or CHA spellcasters

    • DC 12: Gain advantage on the next three spells you cast.
    • DC 15*: Create a scroll of one spell you know of tier 3 or less.
    • DC 15*: Trade one spell you know for another (same tier).
    • DC 18*: Create a Potion of Healing.
