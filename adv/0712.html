Background.

The plague priestesses huddle in their dusty temple that once served a long-forgotten god. Sobs of glorious suffering echo in the halls, and petitioners who are ill enough may touch the fabled Idol of Anguish to merge with those who share their holy rot and torment!

Idol of Anguish.

A jet black statuette of a figure curled in pain. A mortally ill person who touches the figurine dies and transfers their soul into it, merging with dozens of others. This rich cache of souls is worth 500 gp to an evil sorcerer.
