OTHER INFO

The home of the murderous sorcerer Belshazzar holds a terrible secret!

Background.

Belshazzar has put a contract out into the city's dark underworld: Kill you and your companions, payable on delivery. The city's worst thugs and assassins now chase your shadows. The only hope for survival is to cut the contract off at its source. Belshazzar must die!

Hulbazizi.

A green quartz stone carved with a protective incantation. Once per day, wielder can cast protection from evil without a spellcasting check (lasts 5 rounds). Each time, 1:100 chance of summoning a hostile glabrezu.

Map Notes.

Secret Door. Sliding wall panel.

Levels. The trapdoor near the entrance in Area 1 leads to Area 8 (Underground).
