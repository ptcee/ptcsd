Background.

The Emerald Knight has perished, his life crushed by the fall of an ancient oak. His shattered keep has since become an accursed place of ill omen! What secrets and treasures lie buried in the ruins of this abandoned forest redoubt?

The Curse.

A witch used her dying breath to curse the keep with constant ruin and bad fortune. Unless her bones are removed from the well in Area 6 and properly buried, luck tokens cannot be used within the keep's confines.
