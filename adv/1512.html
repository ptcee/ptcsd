The tomb of the Kyzian Prince Kaghan lies open to the sky for plunder!

Background.

Kaghan the Bewitched went to his grave surrounded by splendor in the hopes his malevolent soul would finally rest, leaving his people in peace. But an earthquake has cracked the tomb's ancient seal, and now a wind blows cold and black from within its fabled halls!

Goblet of Kaghan.

A royal blue chalice dotted with sapphires. Once a decade, water poured into it becomes a Potion of Legendary Deeds. The imbiber becomes possessed by Kaghan's tyrannical spirit on each full moon.
