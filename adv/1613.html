OTHER INFO

The Brambles.

Sprouting up in the middle of the Golden Waves, there is a mile-wide warren of black briars so dense and corrupt that no light can enter it and no fire can burn it. This labyrinth of thorny tunnels is the Brambles, home to mischievous spirits, vicious vermin, and evil goblins.

Characters and NPCs.

  • King Krunshlik. The Goblin Boss, wears the Black Briar Crown. Clever, ambitious. Wants to rule a goblin kingdom. Eating human hearts to level up!
  • Mama Yaska. The Goblin Shaman, worships Shune the Vile. Transforms useless humans into wicked, loyal goblins in her bubbling cauldron. Their bites are highly contagious! All standard goblins here are transformed humans (but none of them remember that).
  • Lady Tessa. Scholarly noble on tour. Loves Sir Luke. About to be turned into a goblin.
  • Sir Luke. Knight errant. Already dead, don't worry about him.
