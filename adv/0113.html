OTHER INFO

Greed thrives on rubies and diamonds. Beware when seeking them in the place they call... Molten River.

The Cinder Peaks.

The Cinder Peaks are low, jagged volcanic mountains filled with black glass, sulfur springs, hot rivers, old dwarven ruins, and active kobold worksites. Ignisax the Smoldering, a giant fire dragon, rules from Mount Pyreheart. The Ashbiter and Emberscale kobold clans serve the dragon while competing with one another over engineering, territory, prestige, and survival.

Mount Pyreheart.

The volcano is the domain of Ignisax the Smoldering, a giant fire dragon. His presence makes the region dangerous, and the kobolds create most of the day-to-day activity around it. The mountains also hold remnants of old dwarven works and routes toward Deephome. Heat and mineral runoff from the peaks feed the Argent Run and Brightscale Basin.

The Kobold Clans.

The Ashbiter and Emberscale kobold clans crawl through these peaks, worshipping the dragon as a god of tectonic wrath and setting brutal rockslide traps for thieves. The Ashbiters are smaller and smarter; the Emberscales are stronger and less clever. They share the Obsidian Warrens and are organized enough to field patrols, workshops, engineers, war outposts, and rival clans. They are not just wandering monsters.

Heralds of the Dragon.

Two efreet princes, Malik and Gaspar, hold the Iron Fortress in the hex just south of the mountain. Fire-born themselves, they let the kobolds believe they are the dragon's anointed heralds. Malik sponsors the Ashbiters and Gaspar sponsors the Emberscales, and the clans' rivalry is the brothers' contest for their father's title fought by proxy. The brothers skim the clans' tribute as it passes the fortress and send the gems to the City of Brass. They cannot rob Ignisax openly without breaking the myth the clans believe, so they send outsiders after the hoard.

The Road In.

On the heralds' advice, the clans sealed every surface way into the mountain with rockslides to protect their god from thieves. The only open ways in lead through the Iron Fortress. The Magma Road is an old dwarven tunnel that runs from the fortress's rear gate (Iron Fortress, Area 12) to Area 1 here. A smuggler's route runs up the magma river in Thorgol's rowboat from the fortress's mines (Iron Fortress, Area 35) to the Fire Beach at Area 10. A passage token (Emberscale iron from Gaspar, Ashbiter brass from Malik) lets the bearer past the rockslide traps and patrols of the issuing clan. The rival clan's patrols turn hostile.

Dragon Blood Gems.

The rubies and diamonds scattered through the volcano are crystallized flakes of Ignisax's blood. The Emberscales want them smashed and burned as holy offerings. The Ashbiters want them cut and sold.

Ignisax the Smoldering.

Blood-red scales cover the hide of this mighty, volcanic wyrm. Leaping flames glow at the back of its throat.
