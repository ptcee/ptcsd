OTHER INFO

A guarded treetop city of platforms, rope bridges, and lantern-lit houses, where elk-riding elves keep watch against the dark below.

Runewillow.

Tall silver-barked trees rise like pillars, and high above, an entire city of platforms, rope bridges, and graceful lantern-lit houses winds through the canopy. Soft music drifts down on the breeze, and the smell of woodsmoke and sweet blossoms fills the air. Slender figures with bows watch from the branches. At the base of the city is what is effectively a rune-elk ranch.

The Rune-Elk Elves consider the Emerald Wood theirs and treat outsiders as trespassers. They fire warning shots at any Hunt patrol entering the area. Their greatest concern is the Obsidian Court beneath them, but they also clash with Hunt patrols from Frostholm and with the Blightcreep's corrupted druids as the mires move north. They may have a practical relationship with the Lizardfolk, whose lake borders the forest. They plant bioluminescent mushrooms and moon-blossoms to ward off the vampires.

The Mother Tree.

The city grows from one colossal willow whose runes are carved into its bark. The runes act as a ward against shadow, and the thralls of the Obsidian Court fell sacred trees to break the runic network. The mother tree's roots reach down toward the Court, so the city's foundation is the front line.

Ward Strength.

Track the ward at the Ward Anchor (Area 27). It starts at 6. Each sacred tree the thralls fell takes 1 off. At 3, vampires can cross the glow-ring unharmed at night. At 0, the ward fails and thralls pour into the Rootfold. Moon-blossom raids and cleansing the Blightcreep taint add 1 each.

Law of the Wood.

Trespass ladder. First, a warning arrow in the trunk at eye level. Second, an arrow at the feet. Third, a volley. Fire, hunting for sport, and needless killing are crimes here, not just rudeness.

Entering peacefully. Open flame doused, empty hands, weapons lowered. Wait at the Root Gate to be fetched.

Alarm. Any bloodshed sounds the horn at the Wardens' Watch. A patrol of 1d4+1 Wardens arrives in 1d4 rounds, 16 more in 2 rounds.

Trust.

Track the party's standing: Hostile (0), Tolerated (1), Welcome (2), Honored (3). It rises by 1 for returning proof of the Court's thralls, curing the sick calf (Area 3), repairing glow-ring gaps (Area 7), planting blossoms (Area 12), and cleansing the cistern (Area 28). It falls by 1 for open flame, hunting, needless killing, or a caught lie.

Tolerated: Understory services and the Visitor's Basket. Welcome: the Canopy, rune-arrows, moon-blossom potions, the inn. Honored: a council audience, an elk ride, a rune-antler weapon, the Seed Vault.

Factions.

The Wardens. Hard-line patrol captains who want no outsiders, led by Captain Aldreth Vane.

The Gardeners. Moon-blossom and mushroom keepers, open to useful allies, led by Elder Gardener Sylvenne Dawnmoss.

The Runesingers. Carve and sing the antler runes and the tree's ward, led by Maerith.

The Ranchers. Rune-elk handlers, practical and blunt, led by Elkmaster Taurin Hollowhorn. The Wardens and Gardeners disagree over cooperating with outsiders, and the Council of Three (Aldreth, Sylvenne, Maerith) decides.

Rune-Elk.

Elk choose their riders, and the herd acts as a living defense. Shed antlers can be forged into blades that cut through armor, a reward and a trade item. A sick calf is an early clue to the Blightcreep.

Services.

Moon-blossom (20 gp). Glows as a torch for 1 hour; undead in its light have DISADV on attacks. Moon-lantern (30 gp). A hooded lantern with a blossom lens. Pale Light Potion (40 gp). Drinker glows near range for 5 rounds; vampires have DISADV against them. Blight antidote (25 gp). Cures sickened. Rune-arrow (5 gp each). +1 against undead. Rune-antler weapon (300 gp, 1 week). +1 weapon; target's armor counts as 1 AC lower against it.

Hooks.

Captured Hunt patrol. Three hunters wait in the Wardens' cellar (Area 18). The party can negotiate their release or watch their execution.

Lizardfolk emissary. Stuck at the Root Gate until someone vouches for them.

Missing scout. The Wardens suspect the Obsidian Court took Faeril (Area 22).

Poisoned cistern. Corrupted druids have tainted the water. The party can trace it to the Heartwood Chamber (Area 28).

A thrall in the city. A vampire spy hides among the elves (Areas 21 and 23). A consort who wants to defect is the flip side.
