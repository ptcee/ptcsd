The Bronze Maiden, the Silver Mother, and the Leaden Crone protect the mighty forge that created them!

Background.

The three sisters defend the Cosmic Forge where anything can be made real. Can the characters find a way to use the forge for their own purposes?

Cosmic Forge.

Once every 100 years, the forge can convert a pile of ingots into a magical object of equivalent size. The object can be conscious (see Shadowdark RPG pg. 294-295) and has one benefit equivalent in power to a tier 5 wizard spell (at GM's discretion).
