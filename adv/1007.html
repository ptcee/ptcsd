Background.

The golden swan lays her precious eggs inside a sunlit grotto of caves in the forest. But she is not alone! Fey guardians sent by the Swan Prince protect her and her nest. Can the characters seize one of her eggs and escape with a small fortune?

Golden Swan.

A regal swan with pure gold feathers that glow with inner light.
