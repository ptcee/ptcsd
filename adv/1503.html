Background.

A spring rain has traced the sky with a brilliant rainbow. Everyone knows the legend: a leprechaun's hidden abode lies at the end of it! And if you're fast and bold enough, you can steal the mischievous fey's cache of gold...

Rainbow Door.

The rainbow touches a rocky cave, illuminating a magical door. For two hours of real time, PCs can freely enter and exit through the door. When the rainbow fades, PCs stuck inside must find another way out!

Map Notes.

Trapdoor. 20' deep, DC 15 DEX to avoid, 2d6.

Secret Door. Find if inspect wall.
